A music tracker with a modern DAW inside

Everything here is in the app today. Written in Rust with egui and cpal, up to 32 channels per song.

🎼

Pattern editor

OctaMED-style blocks with note, instrument, volume and effect columns, the classic Z–M keys, selection, transpose and undo. Decimal or hex row numbers and adjustable block length.

🧱

Arranger & automation

Your song as coloured clips with role badges, drag to reorder, click the ruler to move the play marker, plus automation lanes for filter sweeps, levels and delay feedback.

🎲

Built-in generators

Macro-knob instruments: Drum Synth and kits, Analog (Moog-style), FM-4, Noise and Chip FX (an sfxr port). Dice, Mutate, locks, 16-roll history and Bake to sample. 46 presets.

🎹

Synth with Moog ladder

Oscillators with unison and detune, SVF or Moog ladder filter (clean or vintage, 12/24 dB), ADSR, drive, 303 glide and accent, and a tempo-syncable LFO.

🗣️

Voice instrument & Voice FX

Synthetic vowels, babble, stutter and spoken words, a Throat (vocal tract) mode, and Voice Transform: metallic resonance, digital distortion and heavy sub, also as FX devices for any instrument.

🔌

CLAP plugin hosting

Play CLAP instruments such as Surge XT: crash-guarded scanner, browser, native editor on Windows, parameter list, 8 macros, automation, state saved in the song.

🎚️

Effects, limiter & LUFS

Per-instrument FX chain, a mixer with sends and sidechain ducking, a lookahead true-peak limiter and a LUFS meter (ITU-R BS.1770). Exports use the same limiter.

🎛️
NEW

Lower-panel instrument editor

Every instrument edited under the pattern with knobs on collapsible cards, four tabs, A/B compare and parameter search. F11 gives the pattern the full height.

🎵

Demo songs

File > Demo songs: “Lumen Drift” (trance), “Hollow Ritual” (dark psytrance) and “Velvet Mirrorball” (disco with Voice choir), all original. Press Space to play.

🖥️

Themes, incl. Amiga

Dark Circuit by default, or the light Amiga Workbench Gradient theme with classic blue headers and an orange cursor. UI scaling in 25 % steps.

⌨️

MIDI keyboard

Play with velocity, sustain and pitch bend, record step or live into the pattern, and MIDI-learn faders and knobs. Chord helper with scale lock.

📼

WAV & MP3 export

Export the whole song, the current block or an arranger selection, with ID3 tags. Songs save as open .cwt files with autosave, backups and crash recovery.

Also included: sample import with loop points, chop / slice, instrument library, Fill to length auto-arrange, remappable keys and an update check.

NEW IN 0.2.3.19

Sound design under the pattern

The instrument editor lives in the lower panel, so long parameter lists don't push the tracker off screen. It works for every instrument: synth, sample, Voice, generators, Chip FX and CLAP plugins.

  • Knob cards side by side, collapsible, with small envelope, filter and wave pictures.
  • Four tabs: Main, Modulation, FX and Advanced, plus presets and A/B compare.
  • Parameter search with Ctrl+F. Drag a knob, Ctrl-drag for fine steps, double-click for the default, right-click to automate or MIDI-learn.
  • One panel, three jobs: switch between Arranger, Instrument and Automation, and resize with the splitter. Ctrl+I jumps to the editor.
  • Full-height pattern: F11 hides the lower panel when you just want to write.

FAQ

Quick answers. The documentation has the rest.

Is ChipWave Tracker really free?

Yes. ChipWave Tracker is a free music DAW and tracker, released as free software under the GNU GPL v3. There's no account, trial or paid tier. If you enjoy it, a coffee helps keep it going.

Which systems does it run on?

64-bit Windows (installer or portable zip), macOS 11 or later as a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, and Linux by building from source with Rust.

What is a tracker, and do I need to know one?

A tracker writes music as a vertical grid of rows, one column per channel, played top to bottom, driven mostly from the keyboard. ChipWave keeps that fast workflow from OctaMED and adds DAW parts: an arranger, automation, a mixer and knob-based instrument editing. The demo songs are a good way in: open one from File > Demo songs and press Space. F12 shows every key.

Can I use plugins?

Yes, CLAP instrument plugins such as Surge XT, with a crash-guarded scanner, parameter list, 8 macros, automation and state saved in the song. VST3 hosting and plugin effects are planned but not in the app yet.

Can it open MOD or MED files?

Not yet. MED and MOD loading are on the roadmap. ChipWave saves its own .cwt songs, a ZIP of TOML and WAV files you can inspect, and exports WAV and MP3.

Can I use a MIDI keyboard?

Yes. Play the current instrument with velocity, sustain and pitch bend, record step or live into the pattern, and MIDI-learn faders and knobs.

Windows or macOS warns me when I open it. Is it safe?

The builds aren't code-signed or notarised yet, so the system warns about an unknown publisher. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on a Mac Control-click the app and choose Open. Every release has a SHA256SUMS.txt so you can check your download, and the full source is on GitHub.

How do I get updates?

Use Help > Check for updates…, or turn on the quiet check at startup. The download links on this page always point to the newest release.
