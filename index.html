NEW IN 0.2.15.0

Chords that write to the timeline

The Chords, Melody and Bass generators, rebuilt: Write to timeline always puts real notes on a melodic track, tells you exactly where, and only shows up on sounds that can play chords. Every song saves, plays and renders exactly as before.

Write to timeline, every time

  • The WRITE card picks the target: this track, a new track below it, or this clip, and says where the notes will land before you click.
  • A new clip starts at the loop start (or the play marker's bar), and the status bar names the track and bars afterwards. One Ctrl+Z takes it back.
  • Works with no clip open, on an empty song, and from a new Chords… button beside + Add track. Generators guide →

Chords only where they make sense

  • Chords…, Melody… and Bass… are hidden on drum kits, drum generators and drum-named sounds, even a synth called "Trance Kick".
  • That goes for the track pie, the piano roll's chord lane and Generate, and the button beside + Add track.
  • A new Chords, Melody or Bass track always gets a pitched sound, never a copy of a drum.

The song format is unchanged. New in 0.2.14.0: Round Robin, key pressure and clip copy and paste (below). See the changelog.

NEW IN 0.2.14.0

Round Robin, key pressure and clip copy and paste

Loops that sound a little different every pass yet render the same every time, a keyboard that responds to how hard you press, and clips you can copy, paste and loop in a click. Every song saves, plays and renders exactly as before.

Round Robin: patterns that change as they play

  • Step conditions: a note plays every 2 of 4 passes, only the first time, only in fills or never in fills, next to its Chance.
  • Clip variations: up to four takes per clip, one per loop, in order, random, ping-pong or every N.
  • Drum pad round robin: up to four sounds per pad take turns, with a little drift in pitch, level, tone, decay, timing and pan on each hit.
  • Follow actions: launcher clips move on after N loops, and Capture writes the performance to the Timeline.
  • Everything comes from a seed, so seeking and exporting always give the same result. How Round Robin works →

Key pressure and .mid import

  • Aftertouch from your MIDI keyboard (poly or channel) drives a new Pressure source: point it at any knob from its dot.
  • Recorded notes keep their pressure and play it back exactly; draw or clear it in the piano roll's Pressure lane.
  • .mid files from the Browser import as a note clip, with velocities and pressure. MIDI guide →

Copy, paste and loop clips

  • Ctrl+C / X / V copy, cut and paste clips on the Timeline; right-click an empty lane for Paste here, as an independent or a linked copy.
  • A Loop button beside the metronome, and double-click a clip to loop exactly it.
  • Bigger pie menus, with Small, Normal or Large in Settings › Look.
  • Fixes: a drum kit added after StringWave or WaveForge plays the kit, and the go-to-start button is easy to see in Amiga.

Songs that don't use the new features are unchanged. New in 0.2.13.0: live recording, quick modulation and a backlit LCD. See the changelog.

A modern music studio with a retro soul

Everything here is in the app today. Written in Rust with egui and cpal, with up to 32 tracks per song.

🎬

Timeline & clips

Arrange your song as clips on a Timeline with section markers, loop and snap. A drop preview shows where a clip lands, linked copies (×N) change together, and right-click pies keep the common jobs close.

🚀

Clip Arranger

Launch clips and whole scenes per track to try ideas against each other while the song plays, then drag the keepers onto the Timeline.

🎹

Piano roll

Draw, drag and stretch notes, write chords, and generate chord progressions, melodies and bass lines in the song’s key from the Generators panel. Ctrl-drag copies notes; Ctrl+C / V / D copy, paste and duplicate.

🥁

Drum Kit & step sequencer

Up to 32 pads with choke groups, a step sequencer with velocity, swing and 16 to 64 steps, a groove generator, and a library of drum patterns in the Browser.

🧩

Racks & device chains

The Rack under the Timeline: the instrument and its effects in one row, with side tabs, Delete and Ctrl+D on the selected card and drag from the Browser. Split into parallel paths, racks of up to 4 layers, and your own racks saved to My Racks.

🎲
NEW

Instruments with character

WaveForge (polyphonic), the new StringWave string ensemble, Chip FX (an sfxr port), FM-4, Analog (Moog-style), Wavetable, Grid, Noise, Drum Synth and the Drum Kit, all in Browser › ChipWave Instruments. Macro knobs, Dice, Mutate, locks and presets.

🎛️

Synth with Moog ladder

Oscillators with unison and detune, SVF or Moog ladder filter (clean or vintage, 12/24 dB), ADSR, drive, 303 glide and accent, and a tempo-syncable LFO.

🗣️

Voice instrument & Voice FX

Synthetic vowels, babble, stutter and spoken words, a Throat (vocal tract) mode, and Voice Transform: metallic resonance, digital distortion and heavy sub, also as FX devices for any instrument.

🎚️

Effects, limiter & LUFS

EQ, delay, reverb, drive, chorus, filter, bitcrusher, compressor, phaser and more on every instrument, a mixer with sends and sidechain ducking, a lookahead true-peak limiter and a LUFS meter. Exports use the same limiter.

📈

Automation

Draw lanes for volume, pan, filter sweeps and effect settings under each track on the Timeline, or inside a clip so they loop with it.

🔌

VST & CLAP plugins

Instruments and effects such as Surge XT: a crash-guarded scanner, the Browser, the plugin’s own editor on Windows, a parameter list, 8 macros, automation and state saved in the song.

🎛️

Lower-panel instrument editor

Every instrument edited under the Timeline with knobs on collapsible cards, four tabs, A/B compare and parameter search. F11 gives the Timeline the full height.

⌨️

MIDI & recording

Play with velocity, sustain, pitch bend and key pressure (aftertouch), record live onto the Timeline with a metronome and count-in, step-input notes in the piano roll, or play your computer keyboard. MIDI-learn faders and knobs.

🎵

Demo songs

File > Demo songs: “Lumen Drift” (trance), “Hollow Ritual” (dark psytrance), “Velvet Mirrorball” (disco with Voice choir) and “Oceans 11”, a chiptune arrangement of music by Martin Galway. Press Space to play.

🖥️

Themes, incl. Amiga

Slate by default, Dark Circuit, or the light Amiga Workbench Gradient theme with classic blue headers. UI scaling in 25 % steps.

📼

WAV & MP3 export

Export your song as WAV or MP3 with ID3 tags. Songs save as open .cwt files with autosave, backups and crash recovery.

Also included: sample import with loop points, chop / slice, instrument library, Fill to length auto-arrange, remappable keys and an update check.

Sound design under the Timeline

The instrument editor lives in the lower panel, so the Timeline stays on screen while you shape a sound. It works for every instrument: synth, sample, Voice, generators, Chip FX and plugins.

  • Knob cards side by side, collapsible, with small envelope, filter and wave pictures, in the violet look of ChipWave's own instruments.
  • Four tabs: Main, Modulation, FX and Advanced, plus presets and A/B compare.
  • Parameter search with Ctrl+F. Drag a knob, Ctrl-drag for fine steps, double-click for the default, right-click to automate or MIDI-learn.
  • One panel, one switch: Notes | Rack, resized with the splitter, and it keeps one height when you switch. Advanced › on the Rack's instrument card opens the full editor.
  • Full-height view: F11 hides the lower panel when you just want the whole Timeline.

FAQ

Quick answers. The documentation has the rest.

Is ChipWave Studio really free?

Yes. ChipWave Studio is a free music studio and sequencer, released as free software under the GNU GPL v3. There's no account, trial or paid tier. If you enjoy it, a coffee helps keep it going.

Which systems does it run on?

64-bit Windows (installer or portable zip), macOS 11 or later as a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, and Linux by building from source with Rust.

Is this the same app as ChipWave Tracker?

Yes. ChipWave Tracker was renamed ChipWave Studio in version 0.2.4.0, to tell it apart from an unrelated iOS app called ChipWave. Your settings, songs and instruments carry over, the installer updates an existing ChipWave Tracker install in place, and old GitHub links redirect here.

Do I need to know how to use a tracker?

No. ChipWave works like a modern sequencer: clips on a Timeline, a piano roll, a Drum Kit step sequencer and knob-based instruments. For those who love the classic way, there’s an optional Tracker view with an OctaMED-style pattern editor and the Z–M keys. The demo songs are a good way in: open one from File > Demo songs and press Space. F12 shows every key.

Can I use plugins?

Yes. CLAP and VST3 instruments and effects, such as Surge XT, with a crash-guarded scanner, parameter list, 8 macros, automation and state saved in the song. The plugin’s own editor opens on Windows; on Linux and macOS you use the parameter list.

Can it open MOD or MED files?

Not yet. MED and MOD loading are on the roadmap. ChipWave saves its own .cwt songs, a ZIP of TOML and WAV files you can inspect, and exports WAV and MP3.

Can I use a MIDI keyboard?

Yes. Play the selected track’s instrument with velocity, sustain, pitch bend and key pressure (aftertouch), record live onto the Timeline with a metronome and count-in, step-input notes in the piano roll, MIDI-learn faders and knobs, and import .mid files as note clips. No keyboard? Musical typing turns your computer keys into one.

Windows or macOS warns me when I open it. Is it safe?

The builds aren't code-signed or notarised yet, so the system warns about an unknown publisher. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on a Mac Control-click the app and choose Open. Every release has a SHA256SUMS.txt so you can check your download, and the full source is on GitHub.

How do I get updates?

Use Help > Check for updates…, or turn on the quiet check at startup. The download links on this page always point to the newest release.
