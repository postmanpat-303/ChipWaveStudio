# ChipWave Tracker changelog

Newest first. Older screenshots mentioned below live in `dist/`.

**Unreleased: macOS build.** A new manual GitHub Actions workflow (**Actions → macOS → Run workflow**) builds a **universal ChipWave.app** for Apple Silicon and Intel (macOS 11+). It's ad-hoc signed but not notarised, and comes with an icon and Info.plist. The workflow packages it as `ChipWave-<ver>-macos-universal.dmg` and `.zip`, with versionless copies for the README's `releases/latest/download/` links and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. It smoke-tests both CPU halves and the mounted dmg, then uploads everything as an artifact; nothing is attached to a release. **Help → Check for updates…** offers the `.dmg` on macOS, and `--screenshot` no longer touches real settings on a Mac. Install and first-launch notes (Control-click → Open, or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine`): [MACOS.md](MACOS.md). The Mac files will be published with the next release.

v0.2.3.15 adds **click-to-seek on the arranger ruler**. Clicking the bar-number strip above the clips moves the **play marker** to that bar (snapped to the bar line, never before the start of the clicked clip; Alt+click = exact row). While playing, playback jumps there at once and keeps going (tempo and speed are kept; a block loop keeps looping if the target is in its block, otherwise the song plays on from there). While stopped, **Play song** (Shift+Space) starts from the marker instead of bar 1. Dragging along the ruler scrubs. The marker is a triangle on the ruler with a line across the clips and automation lanes; the playhead gets a triangle too. The pattern editor follows the marker; clip selection, clip dragging and the automation tools are unaffected. New `Engine::seek`, `--screenshot --view cue`, unit tests for the bar → order entry / row mapping and UI tests for click, Alt+click, drag and the jump while playing. Details: [ARRANGER.md](ARRANGER.md#play-marker-and-ruler-seek-02315).

v0.2.3.14 (Generators stage 2) adds three generators, all written from scratch.

- **Analog** is a Moog-style synth: three oscillators (saw, triangle, square, pulse; polyBLEP anti-aliasing), each with octave, semitone, level and wave. It has detune, PWM with rate and width, analogue pitch and phase **drift**, hard **sync** of oscillator 2 to oscillator 1, noise, the Zavalishin ladder (vintage by default; LP/BP/HP, 12/24 dB, key tracking), filter and amp ADSR, and glide/legato. Presets: Fat Moog Bass, Psy Rolling Bass, Warm Lead, Brass, Dark Pad, Acid Squelch.
- **FM-4** is 4-operator FM with the **8 DX21/DX100/TX81Z algorithms**, 20 ratio steps, op-4 feedback (averaged, Dexed/MSFA style) and an ADSR per operator. Presets: E-Piano, Metallic Bell, Psy Zap Bass, Growl, Dark Pluck.
- **Noise** offers white, pink (Paul Kellet), brown or crackle through a ladder or SVF with an exponential cutoff sweep, rise/fall envelope, hold, gusts, an impact thump and width. Presets: White Riser, Downlifter, Impact, Wind, Vinyl Crackle.
- All three run their own envelopes (note-off releases them) and have eight `43xy` macros, Dice with knob and choice locks, Mutate, history, seed and Bake (held for 1.2 s).
- New menus are **New… > Generator (analog / FM-4 / noise)**, plus `--screenshot --view analog|fm|noise`. Text songs take `patch = "gen:Fat Moog Bass"` with `[instrument.gen.engine.Analog]` overrides.
- Credits: Minimoog, Välimäki & Huovilainen, Vital (Matt Tytel), Surge XT, Dexed (Pascal Gauthier), MSFA (Raph Levien), Plaits/DaisySP, SuperCollider, Paul Kellet.

v0.2.3.13 adds the **Chip FX** generator, a port of **sfxr** by Tomas Pettersson (DrPetter, MIT) via **sfxr-rs** by Teemu Erkkola (MIT). **Category buttons** Pickup, Laser, Explosion, Power-up, Hit, Jump and Blip use the sfxr recipes; **Randomize** and **Mutate** behave as in sfxr; Dice rolls again in the last category. Every parameter and the wave can be **locked**; seed, 16-roll history and Bake to sample work as for the drums. Eight knobs (Freq, Slide, Duty, Sustain, Decay, LP cutoff, Phaser, Repeat) are `43xy` targets, and all 23 sfxr parameters are editable. The engine is sfxr's (oscillator with slide, duty sweep, vibrato, arpeggio; ASD envelope with punch; resonant LP/HP with sweeps; phaser; repeat; 8x supersampling at 44.1 kHz), resampled to the song rate and allocation-free. Notes transpose relative to C-4. Eight presets: Coin, Laser Shot, Explosion, Power Up, Hit, Jump, Blip, Psy Laser. New menu **New… > Generator (chip FX)**; `--screenshot --view chipfx`; text songs take `patch = "gen:Coin"`. Details: [GENERATORS.md](GENERATORS.md#chip-fx-02313). Help > About links to the ChipWave YouTube channel (demos & promos), and the README opens with the promo video.

v0.2.3.12 adds **Generators**, a new instrument family: macro knobs instead of a patch, with **Dice** (sensible ranges per knob), **Mutate** (±N %), per-knob **locks**, a stored **seed**, a **16-roll history** (plus normal undo) and **Bake to sample**. The first generator is the **Drum Synth**: Kick (v0, 808, 909, Zap psy), Snare (808, 909, modal Shell), Hat (808 metal, ring mod, comb + FM), Clap (808, Tight), Tom (808, Conga) and Perc (Cowbell, Claves, Rimshot, Cymbal, Zap), eight macros each, following the Sonic Pi sc808 and Plaits / DaisySP recipes (independent implementations). **Kit mode** maps eight pieces to the notes of an octave (C kick, D snare, D# clap, F#/A# hats, toms, percs). 22 presets including **Psy Zap Kick** (tuned to E) and **Psy Kit**. New **Moog ladder filter** (zero-delay feedback after Zavalishin; **Clean** or **Vintage** with per-stage saturation at 2x oversampling; LP/BP/HP at 12 or 24 dB) selectable in Synth, Voice and Generator patches; cross-checked against the DaisySP, fundsp and MoogLadders ladders in the tests. New pattern effect **`43xy`** sets generator macro x to y per channel. Text songs take `patch = "gen:<preset>"` with `[instrument.gen]` overrides and `filter_model` / `slope` on any synth patch; generators save as `.cwi`. `--self-test` renders every preset and filter model; `--screenshot --view drums|kit`. Old songs load and sound unchanged (SVF stays the default). Credits in Help > About, the README and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Details: [GENERATORS.md](GENERATORS.md).

v0.2.3.11 adds a **Transform** section to the Voice instrument: three blendable stages after the formants, each with its own amount, and a master **Blend** (dry ↔ transformed; 0 = off, the default, so older songs render bit for bit as before). **Metallic resonance**: six modal resonators (constant-peak-gain two-pole form after Smith & Angell 1982) tuned to the note (± 24 st) or a fixed pitch, with harmonic, metal (TR-808), bell, bar or odd ratios, decay (ring time), damping and stereo spread; the poles always stay inside the unit circle and the output is soft-limited. **Digital distortion**: pre high-pass, drive into hard clip or an in-phase wavefold, bit depth, sample-rate reduction (raw aliasing or filtered), a ring modulator at the note × ratio and a post low-pass; every step keeps polarity, so blending never cancels. **Sub bass**: a sine one or two octaves under the note and/or an octave divider, following the voice's amplitude, saturated for growl, behind a Linkwitz-Riley crossover; below the crossover the sub is clean and the whole voice is mono (side-only high-pass, the side is ≥ 40 dB down below 120 Hz in the tests); the sub joins after the patch filter. **Formant drop** (0–12 st) lowers the formants for a monster voice. **Order**: Dist > Res or Res > Dist. Five presets: **Metal Demon**, **Digital Ghost**, **Sub Beast**, **Cyber Choir**, **Ritual Growl**. New pattern effects **`3Fxx`** blend, **`40xx`** resonance, **`41xx`** digital, **`42xx`** sub (00 = instrument, 01–FF = 0–100 %). The stages are also FX devices for any instrument (**Metal resonator**, **Digital distortion**, **Sub bass**; the resonator and the sub sine follow the channel's note; `40`–`42` set their mix), with 8 device presets. Text songs merge `[instrument.voice.transform]` tables key by key. `--self-test` renders the presets and devices through the limiter; `--screenshot --view transform`. Credits (Smith & Angell, Fletcher & Rossing, Linkwitz, TR-808, analog octave pedals) are in Help > About, the README and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Details: [VOICE.md](VOICE.md#transform-02311).

v0.2.3.10 (roadmap batch 4) teaches the Voice instrument to **speak** and adds **chop / slice** to the sample editor. **Words:** a Voice has a list of up to 16 short words (`ka`, `ta`, `sha`, `dum`, …; typed in the new Words row) and each new note speaks the next one; **`3Exx`** speaks word `xx` (on a held note it says it again). Words are phoneme strings, not text-to-speech: plosives (p b t d k g) are a silent closure, a 10 ms noise burst at the place of articulation and aspiration; fricatives (s z sh f v h) are band-passed noise (z and v voiced); nasals (m n ng) are a murmur that opens into the vowel; vowels glide in from consonant **formant loci** (Delattre, Liberman & Cooper 1955; fricative and burst spectra after Stevens, *Acoustic Phonetics*). A **Speech rate** slider scales the timing (0.25–3×). **Throat mode** (Tract: Formant / Throat): instead of five formant filters, the glottal pulse drives a simulated **vocal tract**, a 44-segment Kelly–Lochbaum waveguide with a nasal branch and a tongue/lips/velum model inspired by **Pink Trombone by Neil Thapen** (MIT; reimplemented in Rust, no code copied); the vowel chips set the tongue shape, consonants close the tract at the lips, tongue blade or tip and open the velum for nasals. New presets: **Talk Ka-Ta**, **Throat Ahh**, **Throat Speech**, **Demon Words**. Throat voices cost more CPU (8 render about 6× realtime on one core). **Sample slicing** (Sample editor, new Slice row): **Transients** finds the hits (sensitivity and shortest-gap controls; slice points snap to zero crossings), **Grid** cuts into 2–64 equal parts; slices are numbered on the waveform, click one (or Prev / Next) to select it so Normalise, Reverse, Fade in/out and Gain work on that slice; Alt+click adds a slice, right-click a marker removes it. **To instruments** turns every slice into its own sample instrument (with 1 ms / 3 ms declick fades); **To notes** also writes them into the current block on the cursor channel at the rows where they fall at the song tempo, so the loop plays back and can be rearranged. One undo step. `--self-test` adds a words / throat / slicing check; `--screenshot` adds `--view speech|slice`. Credits for Pink Trombone (Neil Thapen, MIT notice included), Kelly & Lochbaum, Delattre et al. and Stevens are in Help > About, the README and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Songs from 0.2.3.9 and older load unchanged (Voices without words behave as before). Details: [VOICE.md](VOICE.md), [SAMPLES.md](SAMPLES.md).

v0.2.3.9 (roadmap batch 3) adds **instrument management** and a **chord helper**. Instruments: double-click to rename, **Up / Down** and right-click **Move up / down** reorder slots with every note, lane and render cache following (the song renders identically), a search box over long lists, and right-click **Use this instead of** swaps which instrument the notes in the song (or only this block) play. A new **instrument library** (File > Instrument library…, or Library… under the list) shows the song's instruments, your library folder (`.cwi` files; sub-folders are categories; Settings > Files & Safety sets the folder) and the factory sounds side by side with search, kind filters, `›` audition, `+` add and right-click **Replace sound of XX (keep its notes)**. Instruments save and load as **`.cwi`** files (a ZIP with `instrument.toml` and, for samples, `sample.wav`): right-click > Save to library / Export .cwi…, File > Import / Export instrument, or drop a `.cwi` on the window. **Chord helper** (Song > Chord helper…, or Chords… in the block bar): a song key (13 scales) that you pick or **detect** with the Krumhansl–Schmuckler algorithm (Krumhansl & Kessler 1982 profiles); chord pads per scale degree (triad, 7th, sus2, sus4, add9, power; octave and inversion) that preview with the current instrument; a progression built from pads or 10 presets; **Write into block** as chords (one tone per channel), an arpeggio (up/down/up-down/random, rate, octaves) or a bass line (whole, 8ths, offbeat, rolling 16ths, octaves), one undo step. **Scale lock** snaps typed and MIDI notes to the key (shown as *lock A minor* in the block bar). The key is saved in `.cwt` files and as `key = "A minor"` in text songs. `--self-test` also checks `.cwi` round trips, key detection and chord writing; `--screenshot` adds `--view library|chords`. Credits for Krumhansl & Kessler and Scaler (inspiration only) are in Help > About, the README and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Songs from 0.2.3.8 and older load unchanged. Details: [INSTRUMENT_LIBRARY.md](INSTRUMENT_LIBRARY.md), [CHORDS.md](CHORDS.md).

v0.2.3.8 (roadmap batch 2) raises the channel limit from **8 to 32**, set per song (the **Channels** box in the block bar or the Song menu, 1–32; a text song's `tracks = N`). The pattern editor gets a channel scroll bar under the grid ("channels 9–16 of 32", click or drag; the cursor channel always stays in view, Tab walks all channels). The mixer's channel strips scroll beside the pinned master and limiter panel with their own bar ("channels 1–7 of 32 · drag / Shift+wheel"). The automation **+ Lane** menu groups channel targets per channel (and per 8 channels above 8). Fill-to-length variations (octave, mute, thin, drum detection) now cover all 32 channels; old songs' 8-bit channel masks load unchanged. Undo keeps one step per channel for all 32 (mute/solo toggles never merge). Engine: a muted or quiet channel takes its mixer gain at once when playback starts instead of ramping from 0 dB over 5 ms. CPU: 32 busy channels of the demo render about 4.5× faster than realtime on one core (test `thirty_two_busy_channels_render_fast_and_allocation_free`), and an idle channel costs almost nothing. `--screenshot` accepts `--song file` (.cwt or .cwt.toml). Songs from 0.2.3.6/0.2.3.7 load unchanged; a song with more than 8 channels opened in 0.2.3.7 or older keeps only its first 8. Details: [PATTERN_EDITOR.md](PATTERN_EDITOR.md), [MIXER.md](MIXER.md).

v0.2.3.7 is the first batch of the improvement roadmap. **Master limiter and loudness meter:** a lookahead true-peak brickwall limiter on the master and in every export (default ceiling −1.0 dBTP; gain, ceiling, release, bypass = the old soft clipper), with a **Limiter & loudness** panel pinned to the right of the mixer: gain reduction, momentary / short-term / integrated LUFS and true peak per ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R 128, plus a loudness-target hint (streaming −14, podcast −16, club −9, broadcast −23). Exports go through the same limiter (bit-identical to realtime), *Normalise* now works by true peak, and the export status reports LUFS and dBTP. **Per-row filter commands:** `38xx` cutoff, `39xx` resonance, `3Axx`/`3Bxx` cutoff slide up/down per tick. **303 glide and accent:** per-instrument Glide time + Legato (held notes glide without retrigger), `3Cxx` glide on/off per note, Accent amount + velocity threshold, `3Dxx` accent per note (louder, more open filter, shorter decay); `01`/`02` are unchanged. **Effect fixes:** the Reverb device has a Low cut (default 200 Hz) and High cut (default 16 kHz) on its wet signal (return B shows both); the **fold** distortion no longer inverts polarity, so a dry/wet blend thickens instead of cancelling (100 % wet folds sound the same as before). Engine: automation is evaluated on a fixed 32-frame grid instead of per audio callback, so output no longer depends on the buffer size. Credits for BS.1770, EBU R 128, libebur128, pyloudnorm and the Signalsmith limiter design are in Help > About, the README and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Details: [MIXER.md](MIXER.md), [PATTERN_EDITOR.md](PATTERN_EDITOR.md).

v0.2.3.6 adds the **Voice (glitch) instrument**: synthetic "ahh, eee, ohh, err" vowel chops for dark psytrance, with no samples. A glottal pulse (breath, jitter, tension) drives five parallel formant filters, and the vowel morphs through a sequence built from vowel chips (A E I O U ER AE). There are tempo-synced steps, random babble, Bass/Tenor/Alto/Soprano/Demon registers, formant shift, LFO > vowel / shift, a chop gate and a stutter (beat-repeat) on every note. It is reached from **New… > Voice (glitch)** or right-click an instrument > **New instrument**, with 6 presets: Demon Ahh, Choir Eee, Glitch Babble, Robot Err, Stutter Ohh and Whisper Ooh. The new pattern effects **30–37** set the vowel, sequence step, formant shift, stutter (`33xy`, on any instrument), stutter pitch, breath, babble and chop per row. See [VOICE.md](VOICE.md). The design is inspired by Mutable Instruments Plaits by Emilie Gillet (MIT), with formant data from the Csound manual and Peterson & Barney (1952); the credits are in Help > About, the README and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. **File > Demo songs** lists the built-in demos (Lumen Drift and Hollow Ritual; Help > Load demo song still loads Lumen Drift). The toolbar fix: the MIDI / Chord / Rec indicators move to their own row instead of overlapping the view tabs on narrower windows (about 1600 px with the latency text shown). Songs from 0.2.3.5 load unchanged (test fixture `tests/fixtures/chipwave-0.2.3.5-song.cwt`).
