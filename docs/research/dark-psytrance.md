# Dark psytrance: research notes for ChipWave

Notes from production tutorials, genre write-ups and forum threads (October 2026), mapped to what
ChipWave 0.2.3.5 can actually do. They were written for the extra demo **"Hollow Ritual"**
(`demos/dark_psy.cwt.toml`, generated by `scripts/make_dark_psy.py`). Del's ears override all of
this. In particular the hi-hat is **offbeat only**: one hit on the "and" of each beat, with no 16th hats.

## Key takeaways
1. **The kick and bass are the track.** Dark psy, night-time and forest all sit on a four-to-the-floor kick with a mono
   **rolling bass on the three 16ths after each kick** (KBBB). The bass notes are short, with zero sustain, a fast filter
   envelope that "chirps" each note open by about an octave, and no glide. Make the first bass note (the one in the
   kick's tail) quieter, about -6 dB.
2. **The kick owns the sub.** Below about 90 Hz belongs to the kick, so high-pass the bass just above the kick's
   fundamental. The bass owns roughly 90–250 Hz. Gate or hard-duck the bass off the kick rather than
   using a slow house-style pump: fast attack, about 80–100 ms release, so the bass is silent under the kick and back on
   the next 16th. Keep everything below about 150 Hz mono.
3. **Hypnosis comes from repetition plus slow change.** Write 1–2 bar riffs with heavy repetition, mostly the root,
   and keep to **Phrygian** colours: b2 (a semitone up) and b7 (a tone down), octave jumps, b6 for extra darkness. Use
   call and response between bar 1 and bar 2. Change one note every 8 bars and let the filter do the rest.
4. **The acid sound is a filter, not a melody.** It's a saw into a resonant low-pass, with a separate fast envelope on
   the cutoff (zero attack, about 150–300 ms decay, low sustain). **Accents** (high velocity) open the filter further,
   and slides on about a quarter of the notes. Then sweep the cutoff and resonance slowly over 8–32 bars.
5. **Cuts make the drops.** Mute the kick for the last beat of an 8- or 16-bar phrase. Drop the bass for the last half
   beat. Leave a 1–2 beat silence hole before the big drop. Use high-pass or low-pass cuts on the bass, delay throws on
   the last note before a cut, and accelerating rolls (8ths → 16ths). Strip parts out rather than only adding.
6. **Arrangement works in 8-bar steps.** Make a change every 8 bars and a bigger one every 16. Intro with kick and bass
   only (16 bars), then hats, then snare, then riffs. The breakdown runs 8–32 bars, followed by a build (riser, roll,
   filter opening) and the drop. A dry kick-and-bass reset makes the next section hit harder. Full tracks run 7–9
   minutes; a demo compresses that.
7. **Sound design is dark, cold and metallic.** FM or ring-mod style metallic leads ("buzzsaw"), noise sweeps
   through a resonant band-pass, detuned-saw dark pads with slow LFOs, laser zaps, horror-ish FX and lots of delay. Forest
   psy is the organic, "burbling" relative (148–152 BPM); dark psy runs about 145–155+ BPM. Night-time full-on is cleaner
   and more melodic.
8. **The mix hierarchy and loudness.** The kick leads by a visible margin (kick peaks about -6 dBFS, bass about
   -9/-10 dBFS on a light bus). Keep leads and riffs under the kick and bass. High-pass everything except the kick
   and bass. Pan pads, riffs and FX off centre and keep the kick and bass centred and dry. Send to 100 %-wet returns,
   with the delay return high-passed at about 200–400 Hz and the reverb return at about 400–600 Hz. Mix with headroom
   and no limiter, then master to about **-7 to -10 LUFS integrated** with a true peak of **≤ -1 dBTP**, using 2–3 dB of
   limiting at most.

## Techniques → ChipWave features (what exists in 0.2.3.5)
| Technique | ChipWave feature used | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| KBBB rolling bass | pattern rows 1–3 of each beat; saw `octave = -1`, `decay 0.085`, `sustain 0`, `filter_decay 0.055`, `env_amount` (× 6 octaves) | the first 16th is 8 velocity steps softer |
| Bass gated off the kick | mixer **Sidechain key** = kick, **Duck** 80 % on the bass, release 90 ms | measured: the bass is 50 dB down in the kick's 16th |
| Kick owns the sub | kick tuned to A (55 Hz); bass plays E2 (82 Hz) with an insert **Filter HP 70 Hz** | |
| HP on everything else | insert **Filter (HP)** on hats 10 kHz LP / snare 220 Hz / acid 250 Hz / metal 400 Hz / pad 300 Hz / FX 300–800 Hz | delay return loop HP 400 Hz |
| Acid line, accents | saw, `resonance 0.72`, `env_amount 0.42`, `filter_decay 0.13`, **`vel_cutoff 1.6`** (velocity → brightness = accent), `drive 0.45` | accents = velocity 40 |
| Slides | effect **`02xx`** / **`01xx`** (pitch slide down/up per tick) on the last note of a phrase | no legato glide (see suggestions) |
| Evolving filter sweeps | song **automation lanes**: `instrument.5.cutoff`, `instrument.5.resonance`, `instrument.2.cutoff`, pad cutoff | smooth / linear / step segments |
| Filter cuts | automation of an insert FX param: `instrument.2.fx.1.cutoff` (bass HP 70 → 450 Hz over 2 bars, snaps back) | |
| Delay throws | automation `mixer.ch.5.send_a` (0.12 → 0.6 for the last beat), dotted-1/8 ping-pong return | `mixer.return_a.feedback` also automated |
| Kick drop-outs, silence hole, half-beat bass cut | just notes: no kick on beat 4, `===` note-offs | |
| Rolls | snare channel 8ths → 16ths with rising velocity | |
| Risers / downlifters / zaps | built-in `riser` with level + cutoff automation; `pitch_env` (negative = falling) on a saw + noise; a square with a fast `pitch_env` | |
| Metallic "FM-ish" lead | square + osc2 a semitone up (beating) + **Metal (808)** source + band-pass + **Bitcrusher** | no real FM in ChipWave |
| Dark pad | 6-voice unison saw, width 1.0, osc2 +7, tempo-synced **LFO → cutoff**, chorus | E → F (b2) movement |
| Offbeat-only hat | rows 2/6/10/14, `08 14` per-note decay | Del's rule |
| Per-note hat length | effect **`08xx`** | |

Other effect commands that exist: `09xx` speed, `0Cxx` volume, `0Dxy` volume slide, `0Fxx` tempo / `0F00` break.

## Engine gaps (suggestions, not done)
- **A true-peak limiter on the master / export.** The master is a `tanh` soft clipper and export adds a soft knee to
  -0.3 dBFS. A kick-dominant mix tops out at about **-11 LUFS** before the clipper is driven hard, and LAME then adds
  about 0.5 dB of overshoot (the WAV measures -1.1 dBTP, the MP3 -0.6 dBTP). A look-ahead brickwall limiter with
  4× oversampled true-peak detection, a ceiling setting (default -1 dBTP) and an LUFS readout in the export dialog would
  reach -8 LUFS cleanly. For comparison, 4 dB of `ffmpeg alimiter` on the same WAV measures about -9.4 LUFS. This
  is not a trivial change, so it was left out.
- **A per-row filter command** (e.g. `Fcxx` cutoff, `Frxx` resonance, as already sketched in AUTOMATION.md §4), so
  OctaMED-style users can write sweeps in the pattern. Lanes cover this today.
- **Mono/legato glide (303 slide)**: overlapping notes glide instead of retriggering. `01/02` slides are per row only.
- **A gate on the sidechain** (hold time plus full range) as an alternative to the duck curve. A duck of 80 % with a
  90 ms release works well enough for KBBB.
- **HP/LP on the reverb return**: it has no filter, so only already high-passed channels send to it.
- **Short reverse / reverse-reverb** and **FM operators** would cover the remaining classic transition and lead sounds.

## "Hollow Ritual" as built
E Phrygian, 147 BPM, 3:31 (34 blocks × 4 bars = 136 bars).

| Bars | Section |
|---|---|
| 1–16 | Intro: kick + bass, with the bass filter opening. From bar 13: hats and a pad drone, a kick drop and a bass cut |
| 17–32 | Groove A: the acid enters dark and the filter slowly opens. Bar 32: kick drop, bass HP cut, delay throw |
| 33–48 | Groove B: snare on 2 and 4, metallic call and response, acid variation (b6). Bar 48: roll, kick out |
| 49–64 | Breakdown: no kick or bass. The pad moves E → F → G, sparse acid with the resonance rising, then full acid. The build has a riser, roll and 2-beat silence hole |
| 65–96 | Drop (32 bars): A/B riff variations, the acid filter swinging 0.9 → 2.6 kHz, a cut every 16 bars |
| 97–104 | Reset: dry kick + bass + sparse acid, then a kick-only bar with a roll |
| 105–120 | Final drop with the pad underneath, the acid at its brightest |
| 121–136 | Outro: the filters close and the parts thin out |

### Mix (v2, 18 Oct: reverb, echo and an effects/filter showcase)
Master +6 dB. Sidechain key = kick, release 90 ms.

| Ch | Fader | Pan | Send A (echo) | Send B (reverb) | Duck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Kick | +3.0 dB | C | – | – | key |
| 2 Bass | +1.5 dB | C | 0.15 | 0.06 | 80 % |
| 3 Hats | -3.0 dB | R12 | 0.08 (0.5 in breakdown 2, 0.3 outro) | 0.12 | – |
| 4 Snare | -4.0 dB | L5 | 0.12 (0.6 on rolls) | 0.40 | 20 % |
| 5 Acid | -6.0 dB | L15 | 0.30 (throws 0.9, 1.0 into the pre-drop hole) | 0.30 (0.5 breakdown) | 45 % |
| 6 Metal | +3.0 dB | R35 | 0.35 (throws 0.9, 0.8 in the build) | 0.45 | 45 % |
| 7 Pad | -7.0 dB | C (width 1.0) | 0.10 | 0.50 → 0.7 breakdown | 60 % |
| 8 FX | -5.0 dB | L30 | 0.30 | 0.45 | 40 % |

- **Return A (echo):** dotted 1/8, ping-pong, feedback 0.45, rising to 0.6 in the breakdown, 0.72 in the hole and 0.7 at the end. Loop HP 300 Hz and LP 4 kHz, so the echoes carry no sub. Level 1.5.
- **Return B (reverb):** size 0.93, rising to 0.97 in the breakdown and outro. Damping 0.7 (dark), pre-delay 30 ms, width 1.0, level 1.5.
- **No reverb low cut:** the reverb return has no filter, so the bass only sends 0.06 to it.

### Effects and filters
- **Acid:**
  - synth LP cutoff 200 Hz → 5 kHz, with resonance 0.6–0.92 automated.
  - Insert chain:
    1. HP 250 Hz, swept to 2.6 kHz into the breakdown, the drops and the cuts.
    2. Band-pass 1.1 kHz, resonance 0.65, LFO 0.6 Hz ±1.6 oct, on as "band-pass sections" in the second half of groove A, breakdown 2 and the reset.
    3. Drive (tanh): 0.1 → 0.35 → 0.45 → 0.65 wet by section.
    4. Chorus, wet in the breakdown and build only.
- **Metal:**
  1. Bitcrusher at 8 bit / 12 kHz, 0.3 → 0.75 wet by section.
  2. Foldback distortion, 100 % wet.
  3. Band-pass 1.7 kHz with a slow LFO (0.12 Hz, ±1.4 oct).
  4. HP sweep to 3 kHz.
  5. Its own 1/4 ping-pong delay.
- **Pad:**
  - Synth LFO on the cutoff (8-beat sync).
  - Insert chain:
    1. HP 300 Hz, swept to 2.5 kHz in the build.
    2. Resonant LP 1.3 kHz with a very slow LFO (0.08 Hz, ±1.8 oct).
    3. Wide chorus.
- **Hats:** LP 10 kHz, plus an HP sweep to 5 kHz before the breakdown, the final drop and the drop cut. The outro opens up to 3.5 kHz.
- **Snare:** HP 220 Hz swept to 1.8 kHz on the rolls, and LP 8 kHz.
- **Bass:**
  - LP cuts to 60–130 Hz before groove B, the drop, every drop cut, the final drop and the reset roll.
  - HP cuts from 70 to 450 Hz.
  - The intro filter opens from 110 to 260 Hz.
- **Zap:** 6-bit bitcrusher.
- **Riser:** level and filter sweep.

### Engine findings (reported, not fixed: demo work doesn't change the app)
- **The reverb and delay returns work in export.** A solo acid test with send 1.0 gives wet +1.7 dB (reverb) and -4.6 dB (delay) relative to dry. v1 was simply too dry: its sends were 0.04–0.3 and its return levels 0.7–0.8.
- **Drive "fold" mode flips the polarity** for small signals (f(x) ≈ -x), so a dry/wet blend below 100 % partly cancels: a 35 % wet fold made the metal 12 dB quieter. The workaround is the fold at 100 % wet.
- **The reverb return has no low cut.** The bass reverb send is kept small instead.
