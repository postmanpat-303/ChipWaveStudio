# Chrome Throat: arrangement and sound design

Notes for the psy / dark trance demo **"Chrome Throat"** (`demos/chrome_throat.cwt.toml`, generated by
`scripts/make_chrome_throat.py`; **File > Demo songs > Chrome Throat**). It's built only from what ChipWave
already has: pattern commands, Voice, Generator, synth, FX devices, automation lanes and drag-to-modulate
LFOs. No samples and no new code. It's a different track from "Hollow Ritual" (dark_psy: E Phrygian, 147 BPM,
synth-only).

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo / key | 145 BPM, F# Phrygian (F# G A B C# D E) |
| Length | 41 blocks of 4 bars (164 bars), 4:31 plus the tail, 4:33 rendered |
| Master | limiter on, about -14 LUFS integrated, true peak about -1.9 dBTP (also after a 320 kbps MP3 encode) |

## Brief and house rules
- Keep it simple and rhythmic: repeating, pulsating, hypnotic.
- Four-on-the-floor kick with the classic **kick + three bass 16ths** roll. The Psy Rolling Bass (an Analog
  generator) plays F#1 and ducks hard off the kick. The last beat of every 4 bars turns through G-A.
- **Hats offbeat only** (rows 2, 6, 10, 14). Quiet claps sit on 2 and 4, and snare rolls only come in builds.
- **Audible reverb and echo:** return A is a dotted-1/8 ping-pong echo, return B a dark hall. Echo throws go
  before drops, and the breakdown gets wetter.

## The liquid chrome moment
This is the mirror scene: Neo touches the mirror, the chrome spreads over him and pours into his throat. It's
one **Voice** instrument in **Throat mode** (the waveguide vocal tract): `Chrome Throat`, channel 10. It holds
one note per block and re-speaks words on that held note with `3Exx`, so the tract keeps moving without
retriggering the envelope.

1. **Mirror** (4 bars): an open, sung "aa" on F#3. The drone and Grid pad play under it, with the first glints
   of shimmer.
2. **Touch:** the vowel closes A > O > U (30xy glides). The formant shift `32xx` starts sliding down
   (80 > 64), and the Transform blend `3Fxx` creeps in (10 > 60).
3. **Spread:** gurgled words `gu`, `ugu`, `ngu`, `gugu`.
   - `371A` is a 1/64 chop gate, about 10 Hz: the gargle flutter.
   - `3628` adds babble (the tongue jumping).
   - The metal resonators `40xx` rise (40 > 90), and `32xx` slides on (62 > 40).
4. **Swallow:**
   - `32xx` slides all the way to 00 (×0.5: a tract twice as long, so the formants slide down an octave).
   - The blend goes to FF, which also applies the Transform's 7-semitone formant drop.
   - The sub (`42xx`) rises, breath `35xx` comes up, and half-time kicks and the filtered bass come back.
5. **Pour** (the build): the voice drops an octave to F#2 at full blend, with the resonators at their peak and
   faster chop gates.
   - The kick goes four-on-the-floor again, with a snare roll and a riser.
   - The acid slides open.
6. **Close:** two bars of everything, then a bar and a half of only the gulping voice and the shimmer. A final
   `uk` (a word ending on a closure: the throat shuts) leads into the drop.

The voice's FX chain is automated over the same bars:
- A **Metal resonator** on the note (808 metal ratios, +12 st, 0.9 s ring) for the comb-like metallic ring.
  Its mix rises 0 > 0.75.
- A **Frequency shifter** (both sidebands, 45 % feedback) that slides from 0 to -260 Hz while its mix rises.
  This is the inharmonic gurgle.
- A **12-stage Phaser** with an LFO route on its centre.

The mixer fader lane rides the voice up about 6 dB as it closes, so the darker sound stays present. The test
`chrome_throat_breakdown_renders_and_swallows` checks that the voice alone is much darker in "Chrome swallow"
than in "Chrome mirror".

The **rising chrome shimmer** is a **Wave** wavetable generator (Glass Keys: FM Sweep + Odd Harmonics) playing
an F# Phrygian arpeggio that goes from sparse to 8ths to 16ths to an octave up:
- Pos A (`gen.1`) scans the table, 0.1 > 0.95.
- An upward barber-pole frequency shift (feedback 0.6) rises from 4 to 60 Hz.
- A phaser, plus a high-pass that opens back up for the pour.

## Acid, filters and modulation
- **Acid 303:** a synth saw into the **vintage ladder** (24 dB, resonance 0.78, env amount 2.2 oct, short
  decay), with legato, 70 ms glide and accent 0.7.
  - Riffs use `3C01` (glide to this note) and `3D30` (accent).
  - Each block starts its cutoff with `38xx` and slides it with `3Axx` / `3Bxx`. The squelch blocks push the
    resonance with `39E0`.
  - An automation lane draws the long cutoff arc across the song, and the phaser mix (fx 2) switches on in the
    squelch and second-groove blocks.
- **Drag-to-modulate (`[modulation]`):** an 8-beat triangle on the acid cutoff and phaser centre, a 16-beat sine
  on the drone phaser, a 1-beat saw on the chop cutoff, and a 2-beat sine on the chrome phaser centre.
- **Grid:** a Grid Wavetable Pad holds the breakdown's F# bed.

## Mix and master
- **Gain staging:** kick and bass are dry and centred, with the bass ducked 75 % off the kick. Hats are bright
  (an LP at 13 kHz). The voices get makeup gain from a unity-ratio Compressor, used as a clean gain stage, so
  they sit about 10 dB under the kick.
- **Measured** (`scripts/mix_check.py` on the render):
  - -14.2 LUFS integrated, true peak -1.9 dBTP, LRA 2.4 LU. The 4-bar RMS is flat at -16..-18 dBFS in the
    grooves and dips to -24 in the breakdown.
  - Band balance relative to the total: sub -8.2, bass -2.9, low-mid -8.4, mid -9.2, high-mid -13.4, high
    -13.9 dB. Low end below 150 Hz is mono (correlation 0.98).
- **Limiter:** gain +4.2 dB, ceiling -2.4 dB, release 120 ms. The ceiling sits below -1 so that an MP3 encode
  still stays under -1 dBTP.

## Limits
- The "gurgle" is the Throat tract plus gating, babble, resonators and a frequency shifter. There's no real
  liquid or fluid simulation.
- Voiced closures (`g`) are nearly silent in Throat mode (see VOICE.md), so the gulps lean on the vowels around
  them.
- Voice-specific knobs (formant shift, breath) can't be automated or modulated, so the slow slides are written
  as stepped `32xx` / `35xx` commands every few rows.
