# Tallest Morning: notes

An original uplifting melodic house demo for ChipWave Studio, written by Del (postmanpat).
It is generated by `scripts/make_tallest_morning.py` (helpers in `scripts/cwdemo.py`), and the output goes to `demos/tallest_morning.cwt.toml`.

- **Tempo, key and length:** 126 BPM in D major, 4/4. There are 18 blocks of 64 lines, which comes to 2:17 plus the release tail.
- **Harmony:** Chords generator, EDM / Trance, vi – IV – I – V in D, seed 3, giving Bm7, G/B, D/A and A. Stabs use the triads B4 D5 F#5, B4 D5 G5, A4 D5 F#5 and A4 C#5 E5. The pad plays the same chords an octave down. The outro resolves to D.
- **Loudness:** about -14 LUFS, with a true peak of about -1.5 dBTP and no clipped samples. The limiter ceiling is -1.5 dB.

## StringWave

- **Echo Chord Stabs** (based on the Echo Strings preset) has three StringWave voices, one per track (Stab L/C/R), playing one chord tone each.
  - Echo: about 0.357 s (a dotted eighth at 126 BPM), feedback 0.45, mix 0.3. Echo mix and feedback are automated, so they swell on the builds and throw long on the breakdown.
  - Filter: cutoff is automated (it opens through the intro and builds and closes for the breakdown). The mod envelope also drives the cutoff.
  - LFOs: LFO 1 moves the pan and LFO 2 wobbles the cutoff.
- **Sunrise Pad** (based on the Slow Swell Orchestra preset) has a slow attack and a long release.
  - LFO 1 adds a gentle pitch drift and LFO 2 moves the cutoff over 2 bars.
  - Cutoff and reverb are automated.

## Modulation (Song > Modulation)

| Modulator | Routes |
|---|---|
| Macro "Lift" | stab cutoff, pad cutoff, stab echo mix, lead cutoff, pad reverb. The route depths are automated (`mod.N.amount`), so they rise through each build and snap to 0 at the drop. |
| LFO "Pad drift" (sine, 8 bars) | pad cutoff |
| LFO "Stab sway" (triangle, 4 bars) | Stab L and Stab R pans, in opposite directions |
| LFO "Lead wobble" (triangle, ½ bar) | lead filter |
| Envelope "Lead pluck" | lead filter |
| Velocity "Stab velocity" | stab cutoff |
| Random "Hat drift" | hat pan |

## Mix

- Sidechain pumping: the kick is the duck key. Duck amounts are bass 0.55, stabs 0.45, pads 0.55 and lead 0.15.
- Automation lanes: filter sweeps on the lead, bass and hats; riser level and cutoff; lead delay throws; and reverb size.
