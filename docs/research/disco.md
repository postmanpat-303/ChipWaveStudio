# Disco: reference analysis and mix notes for ChipWave

These notes were written for the demo **"Velvet Mirrorball"** (`demos/disco.cwt.toml`, generated by
`scripts/make_disco.py`; File > Demo songs > Velvet Mirrorball, since 0.2.3.18). Del supplied a reference
recording, which was analysed for style and mix only. **No melody, bassline or lyric was copied.** Every
note in the demo is original. Del's ears override all of this. In particular the hi-hat is **offbeat only, once per
beat, kept quiet**.

### What we took from it
- **Tempo 104 BPM and F minor.** The brief said "about 118–124 BPM (follow the reference)". The reference is a
  103.6 BPM disco classic, so the demo follows the reference. 104 BPM keeps the strut and gives the voice room.
- **Warm balance.** The weight is in the bass and low-mids, with highs rolled off around 6 kHz. Hats stay quiet.
- **Steady energy with short dips.** No long silent breakdown. The breakdown is filtered, not empty.
- **Breathing.** Kick-keyed ducking on bass, strings and keys (about 3–4 dB, 160 ms release).
- **Modern master level.** The demo targets -14 LUFS with a true peak under -1 dBTP, not the 1977 -18.

## Genre rules used (disco / nu-disco)
1. Four-on-the-floor kick. Open hat on every offbeat (one per beat). Clap on 2 and 4. Hand percussion
   (claves 3-3-2, cowbell accents, conga) fills the gaps.
2. Octave bass. Root low then high on 8ths, short notes and no glide, with a pickup into the next chord.
3. Extended chords played as stabs on the offbeats: m9, dom9, maj7, m7. The root is left to the bass.
4. A string section: detuned saws with a phaser, a swell into each section and stabs in the chorus.
5. Electric piano comping in a syncopated rhythm. A lead hook with dotted-1/8 echo.
6. Vocal hooks: "oo / ah / yeah" choir answers, a robot "dis-ko" call, and formant or vocoder-style sweeps.
7. A filtered breakdown (low-pass on strings and bass, a flanger on the break), then a build (riser, clap roll,
   cutoff slides), then the final chorus.

## How the demo maps to ChipWave
Each block is 64 rows (4 bars, 9.23 s at 104 BPM). There are 22 blocks, 3:25 in total.

| Section | Start | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Intro (kick, hats, filtered strings swell, riser) | 0:00 | 2 |
| Groove (bass, claps, perc, EP) | 0:18 | 2 |
| Verse (choir "oo/ah" vowel glides, robot "dis-ko") | 0:37 | 4 |
| Chorus (lead hook, string stabs, choir word hook "yeah") | 1:14 | 4 |
| Breakdown (no kick, low-passed strings, flanger sweep, choir formant) | 1:51 | 2 |
| Build (riser, clap roll, 3Axx cutoff slides, formant sweep 3210→32F0) | 2:09 | 2 |
| Final chorus (everything, extra echo throws) | 2:28 | 4 |
| Outro (strip down, downlifter, filter close) | 3:05 | 2 |
| End | 3:25 | |

- **Drum Synth generators (gen:):** 909 Kick (tuned to D#4, about 62 Hz), 808 Clap, Open Hat, Cowbell, Claves, Conga,
  Cymbal. **Noise generators:** White Riser, Downlifter, Impact.
- **Analog generator:** Fat Moog Bass (octave bass, glide 0) with an automated low-pass and a 40 Hz high-pass.
- **Synth:** "Disco Strings" (5-voice unison saws plus an octave saw). FX: HP, automated LP, a "phaser" (Chorus with short delay and
  negative feedback, slow rate) and a "flanger" (Chorus with high feedback; mix and delay automated on the break).
- **E-Piano** generator with Chorus. **Warm Lead** with its own dotted-1/8 Delay.
- **Voice:** "Cyber Choir" (Transform blend 0.5, words oo/ah/yeah; 30xx vowel glides, 3Fxx blend, 3Exx words,
  32xx formant sweeps) and "Robot Disco" (Robot Err preset, words dis/ko, 32xx formant, 33xx stutter).
- **Pattern commands:** 38xx/3Axx cutoff on strings in the build, 30/32/33/3E/3F on the voices.
- **Automation lanes:** strings LP sweep, flanger mix and delay, bass LP, choir HP, hats HP, riser level and filter, lead
  echo throws (send A), choir reverb and echo sends, echo feedback, reverb size.
- **Mixer:** kick-keyed duck (bass .3, strings .35, keys .25). Return A is a ping-pong dotted-1/8 echo (HP 350, LP 5.5k);
  Return B is a large hall reverb (size .8, pre-delay 20 ms, low cut 250 Hz). Lead, choir, robot and strings send to both.
  The limiter uses +3.2 dB gain, a -1.8 dB ceiling and a -14 LUFS target.

## Mix results (render of 0.2.3.18)
- -13.8 LUFS integrated (ffmpeg), true peak -1.6 dBTP (WAV) / -1.2 dBTP (MP3 at V2). Low end mono (correlation 0.999).
- Bands re total: sub -11.9, bass -2.0, low-mid -7.5, mid -9.7, high-mid -16.7, high -20.0. This is a bit darker than the
  reference, with the hats kept quiet as Del asked.
- Per block: groove, verse and chorus at about -13 to -14 LUFS, intro -17/-15, breakdown -18.5, outro -15/-20.
- `scripts/wet_check.py`: wet/dry -9.8 dB overall (0 dB in the breakdown, -4 dB in the build), wet below 150 Hz at -49 dBFS (the
  low end stays dry), and an audible echo/reverb tail at the end.

## Engine gaps found (reported, not fixed)
- No **phaser** device. Approximated with Chorus (short delay, negative feedback).
- No **frequency shifter** device. The break uses flanger-style Chorus sweeps instead.
- `instrument.N.macro.K` automation lanes drive plugins only. Generator macros (for example Analog cutoff) are reachable only with
  `43xy` pattern commands, so the bass filter sweep uses an FX Filter lane instead.
- `38xx`/`3Axx` filter commands affect only Synth and Voice instruments, not generators.
- Each Voice/generator channel is monophonic, so a choir chord needs one channel per voice.
