# Moonlit Cartridge: reference analysis, arrangement and mix

Notes for the melodic chiptune demo **"Moonlit Cartridge"** (`demos/moonlit_cartridge.cwt.toml`, generated by
`scripts/make_moonlit_cartridge.py`; **File > Demo songs > Moonlit Cartridge**). The brief: a beautiful, melodic
chiptune made with **only Chip FX, the built-in effects and the Drum Kit**, recreating a reference track Del
supplied, with modern touches where they help. No samples and no new code: every tonal sound is a Chip FX
(sfxr-style) instrument and every drum is a pad of one Drum Kit.

## Reference analysis
Del's reference was an untagged MP3 (3:41). Tools: `ffprobe`, `scripts/style_analyze.py` /
`scripts/style_compare.py` and librosa (beat tracking, CQT pitch per 16th, pYIN melody tracking, onset
profiles per band). At Del's request the melodies, chords and bass line were **transcribed and recreated**
(quantized to 16ths); the drums, sound design and effects are new.

## Instruments (all Chip FX unless noted)
Chip FX is tuned for melody: Freq 0.2705 makes C-4 play 261.6 Hz with Follow note on, the sfxr envelope holds
for about 2.3 s then decays, and the generator's amp envelope gives the release (and an optional filter).

| Slot | Name | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chip Kit | **Drum Kit** (the "Chip Kit" preset): kick, snare, clap, hats, toms, crash, shaker, rim |
| 2 | Moon Lead | 25 % square, light vibrato, patch filter, chorus and a dotted-1/8 echo |
| 3 | Soft Square | 50 % square through sfxr's low-pass: the intro, interlude and outro theme |
| 4 | Pulse Arp | 12.5 % square pluck, 16th arpeggios, cutoff automated |
| 5 | Tri Bass | NES-style triangle with a little drive |
| 6 | Phase Pad | saw with sfxr's phaser, slow attack, chorus, low-pass swept by automation |
| 7 | Music Box | sine with punch and a short decay: the chorus an octave up and the breakdown reprise |
| 8 | Noise Riser | noise with an upward slide and a filter sweep |
| 9 | Impact | noise burst with a downward slide, on the downbeats of the drops |
| 10 | Coin Sparkle | the sfxr pickup arpeggio jump, as fills |
| 11 | Echo Square | 37.5 % square: harmony a chord tone under the chorus and bridge melodies |

## Mix
- Kick-keyed ducking (Mixer duck key = Kick) on the bass, pads and arp, kept gentle (a few dB) so the tune breathes
  without pumping.
- Return A: dotted-1/8 ping-pong echo (lead, bell, sparkles); return B: a reverb, low-cut at 350 Hz so the low end
  stays mono. Echo throws on the lead at phrase ends; the reverb opens up in the breakdown.
- Balanced against the reference with `style_compare.py`: within about 1 dB per band from bass to the highs, the sub
  about 3 dB fuller (the octave-drop bass notes), wider in the mids than the nearly mono reference.
- The outro fades every note over 8 bars.
