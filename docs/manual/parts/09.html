CHAPTER 09

Automation and modulation

Automation draws how a knob changes over the song. Modulation makes a knob wobble on its own, in time with the music.

Automation lanes

  1. Open: click the A button on a track header. The lanes appear under the track.
  2. Add a lane: use + lane under the open track, or right-click any knob › Add automation lane. You can automate the volume, pan, sends, instrument and effect knobs, plugin parameters, and more.
  3. Draw: click to add a point and drag it to move it (it snaps; hold Shift for free movement). Ctrl-drag draws freehand. Alt-drag a line to bend it into a curve. Double-click a point to delete it.
  4. Lane header: the dot turns the lane on or off, and × removes it (Ctrl+Z brings it back).
  5. Per clip: right-click a lane inside a clip › Add clip automation to this clip. That automation then moves, loops and splits with the clip.

Tempo changes live on the Song / Master track. Lanes play in your exports exactly as you hear them.

Quick modulation

Every knob that can be modulated has a small dot. Click it (or right-click › Modulate with…) and choose a source:

SourceControls
LFOShape, Speed and Amount: a steady wobble.
EnvelopeLength, Attack and Amount: a movement on each note.
RandomSpeed, Smooth and Amount: wandering movement.
Velocity, Mod wheelAmount only.
  • Speed reads in plain words, such as "1/8 note", "1 beat (1/4 note)" or "2 bars". Untick Sync to tempo for a free speed in Hz.
  • Amount ("+25 %") is how far it moves the knob. Ctrl-drag a slider for fine steps, and double-click to reset it.
  • Each instrument's Movement list shows everything that moves, for example "LFO 1 sine · 1 beat → Cutoff · +25 %". Click a row to edit it.
