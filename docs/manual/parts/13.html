CHAPTER 13

Settings and display

Open Settings with Ctrl+,. Each card shows a short summary; click it (or use the arrow keys and Enter) to open its details. Esc or Ctrl+, goes back.

CardWhat's in it
AudioOutput device, sample rate, buffer size, Play test tone and Restart audio.
LookTheme, style, motion, menus, scale, text size, accent colour and the top display (below).
KeysChange any shortcut: click a key, then press the new one (Esc cancels). A key that's already used is flagged. Reset all goes back to the defaults.
Files & SafetyAutosave, backups on save, the Recovery folder and the update check (chapter 12).
MIDIInput device and your learned controls (chapter 10).
PluginsPlugin folders, Find plugins… and rescans (chapter 11).

Making it look your way

  • Theme: Slate (the default), Dark Circuit, or Amiga (Workbench Gradient).
  • Interface style: Polished, or Flat for a plainer look without bevels and glow.
  • Reduce motion stops the animations.
  • Pie menus: Small, Normal or Large round menus.
  • UI scale makes everything bigger or smaller: Ctrl+=, Ctrl+-, and Ctrl+0 for 100 %.
  • Text size grows only the text, on top of the UI scale.
  • Accent: Theme default, or one of five colours for highlights.
  • Chord menus and Clip menus: choose round (Pie) or ordinary list menus for right-clicks.

The glowing top display

Since 0.2.16.0, the LCD display at the top has a soft backlight glow in the middle and a glassy shine across the top. The text stays easy to read. The glow takes the colour of the backlight you pick.

  • The backlight colours are Mint (the default), Cyan, Amber, Green, Red and Ice.
  • Right-click the display, or use the colour picker under Settings › Look › Top display.
  • Turn off LCD display with the Master in the toolbar to go back to the classic toolbar. The Master then moves under the Clip Arranger.

Moving the top bar's panels

The top bar has three panels: the Display, the Transport (play, stop, record, loop, metronome) and the Master. The middle one sits in the centre of the window.

  • To move a panel, drag the dots at its left edge and drop it where you want it. The new order is saved.
  • Settings › Look › Top bar shows the order. Reset top bar puts back Display, Transport (centred), Master.
  • In a narrower window, the display gets smaller to fit: first it drops the load meters (DSP and BUF stay as text), then it goes compact.
