CHAPTER 04

The Timeline and clips

The Timeline is where your song comes together. Tracks run down the screen, time runs from left to right, and each track holds clips: short pieces of music you can move, copy, loop and split.

Adding tracks

  • Drag an instrument from the Browser (Ctrl+B) onto the empty Timeline. You get a new track that plays it.
  • Click + Add track under the tracks, or Chords… next to it to start with the chord generator.
  • Right-click the empty space under the tracks for the New track menu: Instrument track, Drum pattern, Audio track, Empty track or Browse….

To use a round menu, point at a slice and click. You can also hold the right button, drag onto a choice and let go. Choices marked › open more options. Esc or a click in the middle closes it.

UNDO Every edit is one undo step. The Edit menu names it (Undo: Move clip, Redo: Add note…). Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes.

Playing, looping and markers

  • Space plays the song from the play marker and stops it. Click or drag in the ruler to move the play marker.
  • Shift-drag the ruler to set the loop region, or press Ctrl+L to loop around the selected clips. L (or Loop) turns looping on and off.
  • + Marker or Shift+M adds a named marker (Intro, Chorus…). Click a marker to jump there, drag it to move it, and double-click to rename it.
  • Zoom with Ctrl+mouse wheel or + / −. Fit (F) shows the whole song. Snap sets the grid.
  • F11 hides the lower panel so the Timeline gets the whole height.

Recording from a keyboard

Press Record (●) or R to record what you play on a MIDI keyboard, or on your computer keyboard with Keys → Notes (Ctrl+K), onto the armed or selected track. ChipWave counts in one bar first. You can set the count-in to Off, 1 bar or 2 bars with the arrow next to the Metronome, or by right-clicking it. Loop recording adds layers, and each take is one undo step.

KEYS → NOTES A S D F G H J K L are the white keys from C, and W E T Y U O P are the black keys. Z / X move an octave down / up, and C / V change velocity. While it's on, those letters play notes instead of their shortcuts.

Audio clips

Drag a sample from the Browser's Samples tab onto a track's lane to place it as an audio clip. In the Inspector you can set its gain and fades. ChipWave doesn't record audio yet.

The Clip Arranger

This is a grid for jamming. Each scene (column header) is one section of the song, and each slot is one track's part of it.

  • Click a scene to play it, looping, from the next bar. Click a slot's ▶ to play just that track.
  • ■ at the end of a row stops that track, and ■ in the header stops everything, at the next bar.
  • Launch sets when changes happen: 1 bar, ½ bar, Beat or Off.
  • Drag a slot onto the Timeline to place it there as a clip.

CHAPTER 05

Notes: piano roll and Tracker

The piano roll

The piano roll is the Notes side of the lower panel. It shows the notes of the clip you clicked. Piano keys run up the left side, time runs from left to right, and the Vel lane at the bottom sets how hard each note is played.

To do thisDo this
Draw a noteClick empty grid with the Pointer tool (1). The note is as long as the last one you drew, and dragging sets the length. The Pencil (3) paints a run of notes.
SelectClick a note. Shift+click adds. Drag a box on empty space, or use the Select tool (2). ALL or Ctrl+A selects everything.
Move / resizeDrag notes to move them in time and pitch, or drag a note's right edge. The arrow keys move by a semitone or a grid step, and Ctrl+↑/↓ by an octave.
CopyCtrl-drag, or Ctrl+C / X / V. Ctrl+D repeats the selection right after itself.
DeleteDel. You can also right-click a note, or right-drag across several.
Tidy timingQ (Quantize) snaps notes to the grid.
See everythingF (Fit) zooms to the notes. Hold the mouse on empty grid for a moment to pan.
  • SCALE highlights the notes of the song's key, and Len sets the length the next click draws.
  • Chords shows the chord lane above the notes, and Generate opens the Chords, Melody and Bass generators (chapter 7).

The Tracker (experimental)

ChipWave grew out of a music tracker, the row-by-row editor used for game and demo music. It's hidden by default. Turn on Settings › Show Tracker (experimental) to get the ORDER and TRACKER tabs (Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+1).

  • Each channel has Note, Instrument, Volume and effect columns. Esc turns edit mode on and off.
  • Note keys: Z S X D C V G B H N J M play C to B, and Q 2 W 3 E … play the octave above.
  • The Order list sets the order the blocks play in. Song › Fill to length… (Ctrl+Shift+F) can fill a song out to a set length.
  • Block length: Ctrl+Shift+PgDn doubles it and Ctrl+Shift+PgUp halves it. F9 / F10 mute / solo the channel.
OLD SONGS Songs made in the Tracker open on the Timeline in timeline mode and play the same.
