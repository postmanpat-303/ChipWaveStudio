With thanks to

ChipWave stands on the shoulders of open source and published research. Highlights from the full list (the complete credits are in the About window and in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt):

  • Emilie Gillet: the Voice instrument's design is inspired by the Plaits speech engine (MIT), and the Drum Synth recipes use the Plaits drum models (MIT), as ported to DaisySP by Electrosmith. No speech code is copied.
  • Tomas Pettersson (DrPetter)'s sfxr (MIT), via Teemu Erkkola's sfxr-rs: the Chip FX generator.
  • Neil Thapen's Pink Trombone (MIT): the Voice instrument's Throat mode.
  • Martin Galway: the demo song "Oceans 11" is a ChipWave arrangement of his Commodore 64 music.
  • Sonic Pi's sc808 (Sam Aaron and contributors, MIT), after Yoshinosuke Horiuchi's TR-808: Drum Synth recipes.
  • Vadim Zavalishin, The Art of VA Filter Design, and Antti Huovilainen and Vesa Välimäki: the filters, the ladder filter and the oscillators.
  • Matt Tytel's Vital, the Surge Synth Team's Surge XT, Pascal Gauthier's Dexed and Raph Levien's MSFA: ideas for the Analog and FM-4 instruments (no code copied).
  • The Csound community, Peterson & Barney, Rosenberg, Fant, Liljencrants & Lin, and Klatt: the voice research behind Voice.
  • ITU-R BS.1770-4 and EBU R 128, libebur128 and pyloudnorm, and Signalsmith Audio: the loudness meter and the master limiter.
  • Krumhansl & Kessler: key detection in the chord helper. Scaler by Plugin Boutique: the inspiration for the chord pads and progressions.
  • The Melody and Bass generators draw on ideas from Computoser, Toussaint's Euclidean rhythms, total-serialism, isobar, acid-banger, aciduino, JC-303, acid-test and WalkingBass (no code copied).
  • The free-audio project (Alexandre Bique and contributors): CLAP (MIT), with clap-sys by micahrj. The VST 3 SDK by Steinberg (MIT).
  • Teijo Kinnunen and Ray Burt-Frost for OctaMED, whose workflow ChipWave follows, and the Roland TB-303, whose glide and accent the tracker effects imitate.
  • The fonts Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono by the Braille Institute of America, and Inter by Rasmus Andersson (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
  • The Rust crates ChipWave is built on: egui / eframe, cpal, midir, hound, toml, zip, mp3lame-encoder and more.

Free for everybody, made with love by Del (postmanpat). Thank you for making music with ChipWave! 💛
