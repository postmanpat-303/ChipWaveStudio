CHAPTER 03

A tour of the screen

This is ChipWave with the demo song Neon Overdrive open, in the default layout: the Timeline on top and the piano roll below.

  1. Menu bar: File, Edit, View, Song and Help. The song's name is on the right, with a dot when there are unsaved changes.
  2. Transport: go to start, Play / Stop, Record, Loop and the Metronome. The small arrow next to the Metronome sets the count-in.
  3. The display (LCD): tempo, position, time and status lights. See below.
  4. Master: the master level and meter. Click it to open the Master panel.
  5. MIDI light, Chord, Keys → Notes and Panic. The piano button (Ctrl+K) lets you play notes on your computer keyboard. Panic (Ctrl+.) stops every sound.
  6. Clip Arranger: launch parts of the song live (chapter 4). Ctrl+8 swaps it for the Instruments list.
  7. Timeline header: Follow, zoom, Fit, Bars / Time, Snap, Loop, + Marker and the play position.
  8. Track headers: icon, name, A (automation lanes), M (mute), S (solo) and volume.
  9. Clips: the parts of your song, laid out in time. Markers (Intro, Bridge…) sit above them.
  10. Lower panel: Notes (piano roll) or Rack (instrument and effects).
  11. Piano roll: the notes of the selected clip (chapter 5).
  12. View tab bar: TIMELINE, MIX, FX and SAMPLE.
  13. Inspector and Browser toggles (I and Ctrl+B).
  14. Status line: hints and what just happened. The song length and peak level are on the right.

The top display

  1. BPM: drag up or down (Shift for fine steps), double-click to type, or click TAP four times or more in time.
  2. SIG: the time signature.
  3. BAR · BEAT · STEP: where the playhead is.
  4. TIME: minutes, seconds and hundredths.
  5. LOOP (L) with the loop range, and REC.
  6. CLICK: the metronome. +1 or +2 is the count-in in bars (right-click to set it). FOLLOW makes the view page along with the playhead.
  7. DSP: how hard the audio engine is working. The light turns amber after a dropout. The line below shows the audio status.
  8. MASTER: the master volume and meter.
MAKE IT YOURS The display has a soft glow and a glassy shine, tinted by its backlight colour. Pick one of the colours or any colour you like. Settings › Look has the same choice.
