CHAPTER 06

Instruments and sounds

Every sound in ChipWave is made inside the app. There are synths, drum machines, a synthetic voice and sample players, and none of them need anything else installed.

Finding a sound

Open the Browser (Ctrl+B) and pick the Instruments tab. Drag an instrument onto the Timeline to make a new track with it, or onto a track header to make that track play it. Press Preview at the bottom of the Browser to hear a sound first. The round track menu’s Add › Instrument › ring makes a new track with a built-in instrument.

The Rack and the instrument editor

Click a track and its sound appears in the Rack: the instrument first, then its effects from left to right. The card shows the most useful knobs and a Preset menu. Click Advanced › for the full editor.

  • Macros are big friendly knobs such as Bright, Width, Attack, Release, Motion and Drive. Each one moves several settings at once.
  • ADVANCED › has the Main, Modulation, FX and Advanced tabs. Ctrl+F searches the instrument's parameters.
  • A / B compares two versions. B starts as a copy of A, so edit one, then switch to hear the other.
  • Right-click any knob to Reset to default, Add automation lane, MIDI learn it or Modulate with ›.
  • In the Rack, click a card to select it. Del removes it, Ctrl+D duplicates it and 0 bypasses it. Drag a title to reorder.

Drum Kit

  • Kits… has 13 ready kits: Psy, 808, 909, Techno, Trap, Lo-fi, Chip, Acoustic-ish, Disco, Latin, DnB, Synthwave and Dembow. DICE KIT rolls brand-new sounds for every pad.
  • Click the grid to paint steps, and use the velocity lane underneath for accents. Choose STEPS 16 / 32 / 64, add Swing, and press PLAY LOOP to hear it.
  • + NEW and DUP make more patterns. ADD TO TRACK puts the pattern on the Timeline (the line above the grid tells you where). UNIQUE unlinks a copied pattern, and CLEAR empties it.
  • The side tabs: Pads (click to hear and select, + ADD PAD up to 32), Sound (edit the selected pad), Generate (a new groove by style, energy and fill) and Round Robin (small changes on each hit).

Voice

Voice makes synthetic "ahh, eee, ohh" vowels, babble, chops, stutters and even spoken words. Try the presets, then shape the sound with Formant, Breath, Babble and the Register menu. The Tract menu switches between Formant and Throat modes.

Samples

  • Import WAV (and other audio files) from the Browser's Samples tab or the Sample view (Ctrl+5). WAV, FLAC, MP3 and OGG files work.
  • Slice a loop by Transients (hits) or into an even Grid of 2–64 pieces. Alt+click adds a slice and right-click removes one. To instruments makes each slice its own sound.

Racks, presets and the library

  • A rack is one instrument made of up to four instruments that play together. Right-click an instrument card › Make rack, and use Save rack… to keep it in My Racks for any song.
  • File › Instrument library… holds your saved sounds. Import instrument (.cwi)… and Export instrument (.cwi)… share single instruments.
  • Generators such as the Drum Synth and Drum Kit have Dice (roll new random settings), Mutate (nudge them a little) and lock (keep a knob as it is). Every roll has a seed, so you can get it back.
