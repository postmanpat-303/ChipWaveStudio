CHAPTER 10

MIDI keyboards and controllers

Plug in a MIDI keyboard and play any ChipWave instrument. Its knobs and faders can also drive what's on screen.

Connecting

  1. Plug in the keyboard, then open Settings › MIDI (or click the MIDI light on the toolbar).
  2. Choose your Input device (or All devices). Press Rescan if it isn't listed.
  3. The light is grey when nothing is connected, dark green when connected, and flickers bright green as notes arrive.

The sustain pedal, pitch bend and mod wheel work. Key pressure (aftertouch) is recorded with each note. Press R or the ● button to record what you play into a clip (see chapter 4). Chord on the toolbar spreads chords across channels in the Tracker.

NO KEYBOARD? Turn on Keys→Notes (Ctrl+K) and play with your computer keyboard instead.

MIDI learn

  1. Right-click a control and choose MIDI learn. This works on mixer faders, pans and sends, the master fader, the instrument Volume, Cutoff, Resonance and Drive, and the toolbar BPM. A coloured ring shows it's waiting.
  2. Move a knob or slider on your controller. It now drives that control.
  3. Right-click again › Forget MIDI CC n to undo it, or use the list in Settings › MIDI.

Controller maps

File › Save controller map… saves your learned controls for a controller as a small text file. File › Load controller map… puts them back. The same buttons are under the list in Settings › MIDI.

IN THIS VERSION A map holds knob (CC) learning only, not notes, pitch bend or pressure. Loading a map replaces the learned list.

MIDI files

Your .mid files appear in the Browser. Drag one onto a track, or double-click it, and it comes in as a note clip.
