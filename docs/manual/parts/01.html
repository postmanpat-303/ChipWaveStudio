CHAPTER 01

Welcome and installing

ChipWave Studio is a free music studio for your computer. You arrange songs on a Timeline and play them with built-in synths, drum kits, a synthetic voice and samples. Generators write chords, melodies and bass lines with you, and a mixer with effects and a loudness meter gets the song ready to share. If you like old-school trackers, there's a Tracker under the hood too.

It's open source (GPL-3.0-or-later), and it runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. There are no accounts, no subscriptions and no ads.

NEW IN 0.2.18.0
  • The Arp: add it to a track's Rack and it plays the notes you hold one at a time, in time with the song, with modes, octaves, rates, a step grid of up to 32 steps, Latch and presets.
  • An adjustable loop: drag the ends of the loop on the ruler to resize it, or drag its band to move it.
  • Ctrl+drag a clip to copy it. The original stays where it was.
  • A new demo song, "Tallest Morning" (File › Demo songs).
Your songs open and play just as they did in 0.2.17.0.

Windows

  1. Go to github.com/postmanpat-303/ChipWaveStudio/releases/latest.
  2. Download ChipWave-setup-x64.exe (installer) or ChipWave-windows-x64.zip (portable: unzip it anywhere and run it).
  3. Run it. ChipWave isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer)

  1. From the same releases page, download ChipWave-macos-universal.dmg (or the zip) and drag ChipWave into Applications.
  2. The app isn't notarised yet, so the first time, Control-click it and choose Open. If macOS still refuses, run this in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ChipWave.app
  3. Shortcuts shown as Ctrl also work with ⌘ Cmd (⌘S saves, ⌘Z undoes).

Linux

Build from source. Install Rust (from rustup.rs) and the ALSA development package (libasound2-dev on Debian and Ubuntu), then in the project folder run:

cargo run --release

First start

ChipWave opens with a clean song on the Timeline. For a quick taste, choose File › Demo songs, pick one and press Space. The first time, ChipWave also offers to find plugins. You can scan automatically, choose folders, or skip (see chapter 11).

UPDATES Help › Check for updates… tells you when a new version is out. You can also turn on Check for updates at startup in Settings › Files & Safety.

CHAPTER 02

Your first song in 10 minutes

This walk-through builds a short loop with drums, chords, bass and a melody, then exports it as an MP3. Don't worry about getting it "right": everything can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

  1. Start fresh. Choose File › New (Ctrl+N). You get an empty Timeline with only the Song / Master track. 0:30
  2. Add drums. Press Ctrl+B to show the Browser. On the Instruments tab, drag Drum Kit onto the empty Timeline. In the Drum Kit editor below, click DICE KIT until you like the sounds, paint a few steps, then click ADD TO TRACK. 2:00
  3. Add a chord instrument. Drag WaveForge (or Synth) from the Browser onto the Timeline. A new track appears. 3:00
  4. Write chords. Click Chords… under the tracks. Pick a Style (try Synthwave) and a Key, press Preview, and click Another one until it sounds good. Then click Write to timeline. 5:00
  5. Add a bass line. In the same generator panel, open the Bass tab, pick a style (for example Octave pump) and write it. It goes on a new "Bass" track. 6:00
  6. Add a melody. Open the Melody tab, try a few Another one rolls, and write it to a new "Melody" track. 7:00
  7. Listen. Press Space to play and stop. Press L to turn looping on or off, or Shift-drag the ruler to choose the loop. 7:30
  8. Balance it. Open the Mixer (Ctrl+3 or the MIX tab) and pull the faders until nothing is too loud. The master limiter keeps peaks safe. 8:30
  9. Save. Press Ctrl+S and give your song a name. It's saved as a .cwt file. 9:00
  10. Export. Press Ctrl+Shift+E, choose MP3 and click Export. Your song lands in Music/ChipWave. Done! 10:00
NO SOUND? Check Settings › Audio: choose your output device and click Play test tone. Chapter 15 has more help.
