CHAPTER 08

Mixer and effects

The Mixer (Ctrl+3) gives every track a channel strip. The FX view (Ctrl+4) shows the effect chain of the selected instrument.

  1. Sidechain key / release: pick the track that "pumps" the others (usually the kick).
  2. + Insert adds an effect to the channel.
  3. A DLY / B REV send the channel to the shared delay and reverb.
  4. Pan, and Duck (how much this channel dips when the key track plays).
  5. Fader and meter. Double-click the fader for 0 dB.
  6. M / S: mute and solo (also F9 / F10).
  7. Master strip, the final output.
  8. Limiter & loudness: the true-peak limiter (gain, ceiling, release) and LUFS loudness meters.
  9. Target shows how your loudness compares with a goal (streaming −14, podcast −16, club −9 or broadcast −23).
TIP The limiter is on by default, and exports go through it too, so your WAV or MP3 sounds just like playback. The Master readout in the toolbar's LCD opens the Master panel.

Effects

Use + Device to add an effect, or drag one from the Browser's Effects tab onto a track or into the Rack. Every effect card has a power button (bypass), a folder icon for presets, and its own Mix knob.

EffectWhat it does
DelayEchoes, synced to the tempo or set in ms, with ping-pong.
ReverbRooms and halls, with pre-delay and width.
DriveSoft, hard or fold distortion.
Chorus / FlangerThickens and swirls the sound.
FilterLow-pass, high-pass or band-pass, with an LFO.
EQ4 bands: low shelf, two peaks and a high shelf.
CompressorEvens out the level.
BitcrusherLo-fi bit depth and sample-rate reduction.
PhaserA sweeping, whooshing phase effect.
Frequency shifterShifts every frequency up or down for metallic, detuned effects.
Metal resonator, Digital distortion, Sub bassThe Voice instrument's Transform stages, usable on any sound.
Plugin effectAny installed CLAP or VST3 effect (see chapter 11).

A Split sends the sound down 2 to 4 parallel paths and mixes them back together. Add it from + Add Device in the chain editor (right-click a Rack card › Open in the chain editor).
