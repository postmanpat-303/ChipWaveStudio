CHAPTER 07

Chords, Melody and Bass generators

Stuck for ideas? The generators write chord progressions, melodies and bass lines in the style you pick. Keep rolling until something clicks, then write it onto the Timeline.

Open them with Generate in the piano roll, Chords… next to + Add track, or a track's right-click menu (Chords…). Drag the ⠿ grip to move the panel, and double-click the grip to put it back.

  1. Chords · Melody · Bass tabs.
  2. The progression: the key and chords, shown as coloured pads. Click a pad to hear it.
  3. Another one rolls a new progression, and Preview plays it.
  4. Style: Cinematic, Ambient, Indie Pop, Emo, Pop, Lo-fi / Neo Soul, Synthwave, EDM / Trance, Dark / Horror, Folk / Country, Blues, Heroic / Game, Dark psy, House, Jazz, Rock and Classical.
  5. Key: C to B, with Any, Major or Minor.
  6. Length (4, 8, 16 or 32 chords) and Chord speed. The Colour and Variation sliders add spice, and Walking bass adds inversions.
  7. Target: this track, a new track, or this clip.
  8. Write: Replace notes, Notes on top, or Markers only. Pick a Voicing (Full, Triads, Shell, Bass only, Pad, Light, Open or Power) and an Octave.
  9. Write to timeline. The card tells you exactly what will happen, and one Ctrl+Z takes it back.
GOOD TO KNOW Chords only go onto pitched sounds, so the Chords tab is hidden on drum tracks. If more than 8 notes would be replaced, ChipWave asks first.

Melody

  • The melody follows your chords: either the Chords tab or the clip's chord lane (Over).
  • Preview, Dice and Another one try new ideas. The Seed number brings back a favourite.
  • Rhythm: Quarters, Eighths, Syncopated, Sixteenths, Long notes or Euclid. Contour: Arch, Rise, Fall, Wave or Walk. Motif: Off, AABA, ABAB or Call & response.
  • Density, Range and Octave shape it. Lock rhythm or Lock notes keeps one part while you re-roll the other. Advanced… has more options, such as End on tonic.
  • By default it writes to a new "Melody" track.

Bass

  • Styles: Root, Octave pump, Walking, Acid, Syncopated and Euclid, with Density, Range and Octave.
  • It writes to a new "Bass" track. It copies the current track's sound. If that track has no instrument or plays drums, you get a bass synth instead (Fat Moog Bass, or Acid Squelch for Acid).

The chord lane

In the piano roll, Chords shows a chord lane above the notes. Double-click it to add a chord, or drag to draw one. Click a chord to hear it, and press Del to delete it. Right-click an empty spot on the lane for a round menu of styles and progressions, or right-click a chord to play, transpose, change its type, see suggestions, write its notes or edit it. If you prefer plain lists, choose Settings › Look › Chord menus › List. The list also has Detect chords from the notes.
